Apartment Move-Out Cleaning Checklist

Practical, location-neutral planning help for renters preparing to clean, document, budget, and return keys.

Use this room-by-room checklist to clean in a practical order and prepare for final photos. Check your lease or building instructions before doing repairs or specialty cleaning.

Kitchen

  • Empty and wipe cabinets, drawers, pantry shelves, counters, and backsplash.
  • Clean oven, stovetop, microwave, dishwasher edges, refrigerator shelves, freezer, sink, and faucet.
  • Pull out crumbs where safely reachable and sweep or mop under movable items.
  • Remove food, trash, liners, magnets, tape, and personal items.

Bathroom

  • Scrub toilet, sink, tub or shower, fixtures, mirror, vanity, medicine cabinet, and fan cover.
  • Remove soap residue, hair, personal products, shower liners, and trash.
  • Wipe baseboards, floor edges, towel bars, shelves, and inside drawers.

Bedrooms and closets

  • Empty closets, shelves, hooks, and drawers.
  • Dust ceiling fan blades, light fixtures, windowsills, and baseboards.
  • Remove tape, temporary hooks, and residue carefully.
  • Vacuum carpet or mop hard floors after furniture is moved.

Living areas, floors, and windows

  • Wipe switches, outlets, doors, handles, trim, and visible wall marks.
  • Vacuum slowly, mop hard floors, and follow lease instructions for carpet cleaning if any.
  • Dust blinds, wipe reachable window glass, clean tracks, and report pre-existing damage if needed.

Final pass

  • Clear balcony, porch, storage cage, mailbox, garage, and assigned parking area.
  • Take final dated photos or video after cleaning and after furniture is out.
  • Return all keys, fobs, remotes, and passes as instructed.
  • Save receipts and notes in one move-out folder.
