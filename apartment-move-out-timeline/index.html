A simple timeline can make move-out week less chaotic. Adjust this schedule for your lease, move date, building rules, and whether you are moving locally or long-distance.

30 days before move-out

  • Review notice requirements, move-out instructions, cleaning expectations, and key/fob return rules.
  • Book movers, truck rental, elevator reservations, parking permits, or loading dock time if needed.
  • Start a move-out folder for receipts, photos, lease notes, utility confirmations, and messages.
  • Use the move-out calculator to estimate cleaning, supplies, truck, utility, and disposal costs.

2 weeks before move-out

  • Declutter, donate, sell, or schedule disposal for items you are not taking.
  • Buy cleaning supplies, boxes, tape, labels, trash bags, and minor supplies your lease allows.
  • Schedule professional cleaning, carpet cleaning, repair help, or junk removal if you plan to use them.
  • Confirm how final inspection and forwarding-address steps work.

1 week before move-out

  • Clean low-use rooms, closets, storage areas, windowsills, and baseboards.
  • Pack nonessential items and label boxes by destination room.
  • Take first photos of rooms, appliances, floors, and any areas you want documented.
  • Confirm utility transfer, internet equipment return, and final bill timing.

Final day

  • Finish kitchen, bathroom, floors, trash, fridge, freezer, and entry areas.
  • Walk every room and open every drawer, closet, cabinet, and storage area.
  • Take final dated photos or video after everything is out.
  • Return keys, fobs, remotes, passes, and provide forwarding information if needed.
  • Save receipts, photos, and written communication after you leave.
